# Support FAQ — Northwind Analytics

**How do I reset my dashboard password?**
Use "Forgot password" on the login page; a reset link is emailed and is valid for 30 minutes.

**What is the data refresh interval?**
Dashboards refresh every **15 minutes** on the Standard plan and every **1 minute** on Pro.

**What does error ERR-4032 mean?**
ERR-4032 means the data source connection timed out. Retry after 60 seconds; if it persists,
check the source credentials in Settings > Connections.

**How long is analytics data retained?**
Analytics data is retained for **13 months** on Standard and **36 months** on Pro.
