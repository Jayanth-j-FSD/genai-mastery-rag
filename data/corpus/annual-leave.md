# Annual Leave Policy

Full-time employees at Northwind Labs accrue **24 days** of paid annual leave per year,
accrued monthly at 2 days per month.

## Carry-over
Up to **5 unused leave days** may be carried into the following year. Any unused days
beyond 5 are forfeited on 31 December and are not paid out.

## Booking
Leave must be requested at least **two weeks** in advance through the HR portal and
approved by your line manager. Requests during the December code-freeze are not guaranteed.
