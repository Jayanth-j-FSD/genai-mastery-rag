# Expenses Policy

## Submitting
Submit expenses within **30 days** of purchase through the finance portal with a receipt.
Expenses over **$200** require pre-approval from your manager.

## Limits
- Meals while travelling: up to **$40 per day**.
- Home-office equipment: up to **$500 per year**.
- Client entertainment: up to **$150 per event**, itemised receipt required.

Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll run.
