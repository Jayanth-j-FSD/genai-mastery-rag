# Week 5 — the question bank used to build a population of real traces.
# One question per line. Lines starting with # and blank lines are ignored.
#
# Run it with:  python scripts/ask_batch.py
# Every question goes through the normal answer path, so each one is logged as a
# trace. Week 5 then draws a random sample from those traces.
#
# Why more questions than the 20 you need: sampling 20 out of 20 is not sampling.
# A larger population is what makes the random draw meaningful, which is exactly
# what the mentor checks first ("did they read a fair sample?").
#
# The mix is deliberate — clear questions, questions whose answer is in two
# documents, questions that are not in the corpus at all, and vague questions a
# real user would actually type. The vague ones are where the interesting
# failures live.

# --- leave ---
How many annual leave days do full-time employees get?
How many unused leave days can I carry over to next year?
What happens to unused leave days on 31 December?
How far in advance do I have to request leave?
Who approves my leave request?
Can I take leave during the December code-freeze?

# --- remote work ---
How many days a week can I work remotely?
How many days must I be in the office?
Which day is the company-wide collaboration day?
What do I need for a fully remote arrangement?
How often is a fully remote arrangement reviewed?

# --- benefits ---
What is the pension match?
When does the pension match start?
What is the annual learning budget?
Is health insurance covered for my dependents?
When do my benefits begin?

# --- expenses ---
What is the daily meal limit while travelling?
How much can I claim for home-office equipment?
What is the limit for client entertainment?
When does an expense need pre-approval?
How long do I have to submit an expense?
When are reimbursements paid?

# --- security ---
How long must my password be?
How often must passwords be rotated?
Is MFA mandatory?
Who do I report a security incident to?
How quickly must I report a security incident?
Can I copy customer data to my personal cloud storage?

# --- product support ---
What does error ERR-4032 mean?
How long is a password reset link valid?
What is the data refresh interval on the Pro plan?
How long is analytics data retained on the Standard plan?
What should I check in Settings > Connections?

# --- not in the corpus: the app should refuse these ---
What is the company's parental leave policy?
How many sick days do employees get?
What is the notice period for resignation?
Is there an annual bonus scheme?
What is the dress code?

# --- vague, the way real users ask ---
What are my spending limits?
What are the rules about time off?
Tell me about security.
What is the policy?
How much can I claim?
